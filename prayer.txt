**A Prayer of Surrender**

Take, Lord, the weight of my heart,
the burden of my sorrow,
and the ache of my longing.
All I have known as pain and grief,
I give to you.

You are the comforter of the downtrodden,
the healer of everything that is broken,
and the quiet shelter in the storm.

Give me not a sense of control or power,
but a deep trust in your goodness,
and a surrender of all my plans.

Let my heart, once heavy with sorrow,
now be lifted by your love and care.
Fill my soul with the calm of your presence,
and give me peace that surpasses all understanding.

You are the One who knows the depth of my pain,
and yet you are always with me,
to walk beside me through the darkness,
and to bring light to the shadows.

Give me, Lord, a heart that trusts in you,
a mind that submits to your will,
and a spirit that is rooted in hope.
This is enough for me.
